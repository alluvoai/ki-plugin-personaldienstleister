# Structure, industry templates, and channel formats

## Standard structure (Talent Hub, website, full-text job boards)

1. **Title**: position (m/w/d), location, working-time model. Example:
   „Pflegefachkraft (m/w/d) – Köln – Vollzeit oder Teilzeit". Under 80 characters.
2. **Opening** (2–4 sentences): who is hiring, for what, and what makes the role special. A
   concrete detail beats every cliché. Keep the form of address consistent from the first sentence.
3. **Responsibilities** (4–7 points): lead with verbs, be concrete, and do not list the entire profession.
4. **Profile** (required, then preferred): qualifications, experience, language, driving licence,
   and availability. Mark preferred items clearly as „schön, aber kein Muss".
5. **What we offer** (5–8 points): compensation with the number first, then allowances, security
   (unbefristet), time (schedule, holiday), and extras. Use only confirmed items.
6. **Framework**: placement form (as a separate sentence for Arbeitnehmerüberlassung), location
   or region, start, fixed-term status, and working time.
7. **Application and contact**: how (link, WhatsApp, email, phone), to whom by name, how quickly
   a reply comes („innerhalb von zwei Werktagen"), and the data-protection notice.

Length: 300–500 words for the full text. More than 600 will not be read.

## Industry templates (accents, not replacements for the standard structure)

**Pflege und Medizin.** Put the Fachweiterbildung in the title when required („Pflegefachkraft
Intensiv"). Lead with schedule benefits: Wunschdienstplan, free weekends, no last-minute cover.
State allowances concretely. Use a travel radius instead of naming the client hospital. Name
possible areas of work (ITS, OP, Anästhesie, Geriatrie).

**Industrie und Produktion.** Put the shift model in the title (2-Schicht, 3-Schicht, Konti).
Hourly pay plus allowances. State requirements honestly (standing, lifting, Staplerschein).
Mention a takeover opportunity only when realistic.

**Logistik und Lager.** Put the start date and location first; many applicants search by district.
Clearly separate driving licence and Staplerschein as required or preferred. Give working times
with clock times.

**Büro, kaufmännisch, Verwaltung.** Make responsibilities precise (which systems, which processes),
state the home-office rule concretely („zwei Tage pro Woche"), and mention training.

**Handwerk und Technik.** Put the trade and Gesellenbrief or Meister in the title. Cover tools,
vehicle, installation allowances, and the travel-away arrangement.

## Channel formats (generate only on request)

**Indeed.** Title ≤ 80 characters without chains of special characters, full text with the standard
sections as headings, salary range in Indeed's salary field, and location as city with postcode.

**Bundesagentur für Arbeit (Jobbörse).** Fields: job title, Tätigkeit (professional title under
the classification), location, start, fixed-term status, working time, compensation, requirements,
and application route. No marketing copy; keep it factual.

**Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram).** Primary text ≤ 125 characters, headline ≤ 40 characters,
description ≤ 30 characters, call to action („Jetzt bewerben"). Three variants with different
hooks (salary, schedule, team). The landing page is the Talent Hub job.

**Social post (LinkedIn, Facebook page).** 3–5 sentences in first-person singular or plural, one
hashtag sentence, and a link. More personal than the posting.

**WhatsApp short version.** 5–8 lines: position, location, time, pay, one benefit, and contact.
For referral programmes and reactivating former applicants.

## File format

Markdown, with an H1 title on the first line and the sections as H2 headings afterwards. End
with a comment block `<!-- Compliance-Check: … -->` containing the findings table so it remains
traceable without appearing when published.
